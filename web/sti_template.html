STI book with a capped yield tree

The portfolio is a Straits Times basket. Two monthly questions pick a factor mix. The mix only nudges weights, and each nudge is capped.

How the STI weight is computed

The published Straits Times Index holds about 30 companies. A company's index weight is its price times its free-float shares. The index level is that total divided by a divisor, so the level does not jump when the share count changes.

This page uses the 30 names in the Straits Times Index as of October 2026. The same 30 are used at every past review. The share count is today's market value divided by the latest price. On a review close, weight equals that day's price times the share count, and the 30 weights are scaled to 100 percent.

The basket is reset on the last trading day on or before the third Friday of March and of September. Between those reviews the weights move with prices. A new listing or a large corporate action can change the published STI between reviews. This basket waits for the next March or September date.

The two questions

Each month answers two questions. A review uses the previous month's answers, then keeps that mix until the next review.

Question 1. Government yield10-year SGSThe MAS benchmark yield for the 10-year Singapore government bond. Above the median of earlier months means high. Below it means low. The median uses up to 120 earlier months, and at least 36.
Question 2. CreditHigh yield minus investment gradeHigh yield is iShares USD Asia High Yield (O9P). Investment grade is Amova SGD Investment Grade (MBH), formerly Nikko. Each fund's yield is the dividends it paid over the last 365 days, divided by its price. Tiger supplies both price histories, from December 2019.

Credit is the high-yield fund's yield minus the investment-grade fund's yield. A larger gap means the riskier bonds pay more extra yield. That month is wide. A smaller gap is tight. Wide and tight use the same median rule as the 10-year yield.

The tree

Start at the top. The first branch is the 10-year yield. Each of those branches then splits on credit. The blue path is the leaf on the selected close, and the mix under that leaf is the one chosen on Singapore.

Features

A missing sector score counts as zero. The leaf chooses the four weights in the score. A stock is compared with the other stocks in its own sector, not with the whole market.

FeatureWhat it measuresFormula
ValueHow cheap the stock is. The book figure waits 40 days after the quarter-end, then is carried forward.Book per share / Price
DividendThe last cash dividend, carried forward after its payment date. One payment, not a full-year yield.Last dividend / Price
Low volatilityHow calm the recent price has been. A calmer stock scores higher.1 / σ of the last 30 daily returns
MomentumHow often the stock closed up recently. It is a count of up days, not a twelve-month return.Number of positive returns in the last 45 sessions
Sector scoreThe stock versus the other names in its sector, for one feature.z = (feature − sector mean) / sector standard deviation
ScoreThe leaf’s mix of those four sector scores. Weights are momentum, value, dividend, low volatility.wm zm + wv zv + wd zd + wlv zlv
10-year yieldThe MAS 10-year Singapore government yield. Above the median of earlier months is high.High if yield > median of up to 120 earlier months
Bond-fund yieldIncome from the high-yield fund, or from the investment-grade fund. Tiger prices, dividends over a trailing year.Dividends over the last 365 days / Price
CreditHow much extra yield the riskier bonds pay. Above its own median is wide.High-yield fund yield − investment-grade fund yield
STI weightThe company’s share of the basket. Shares are today’s market value divided by the latest price.Price × shares / Σ (price × shares)
Active weightExtra weight for a stock whose score is above the index-weighted average score. Capped at 1 percentage point, and funded by trimming the other names.scale × index weight × max(score − average, 0)
Predicted tracking errorHow large the yearly gap versus the index is expected to be. Scale is cut until this is inside 1.25%.√(a′ Σ a) × √252
Information ratioThe gap versus the index, divided by how unsteady that gap is. Each leaf keeps the mix with the highest ratio on its own sessions.mean(book − index) / σ(book − index) × √252

σ is the standard deviation. Σ is the covariance of the last 252 daily returns known on the review close. a is the column of active weights. The review uses the previous month’s yield answers.

From the leaf to the book

The hurdle is the index-weighted average score. A stock above the hurdle receives the active weight in the table. Stocks at or below the hurdle are trimmed so those extras are fully funded. After the review, prices can carry a weight a little past the 1 point cap. The book then waits for the next March or September review.
